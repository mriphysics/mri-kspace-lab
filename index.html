What should I notice?

Centre

Contrast & broad structure

The bright centre of k-space holds low spatial frequencies: the slowly varying shapes and most signal energy.

Edges

Sharp detail

Outer k-space contains rapid spatial changes. Cropping it keeps the anatomy recognisable but softens boundaries.

Spacing

Field of view

Increasing the gap between sampled lines shrinks encoded FOV. Anatomy outside it folds back into the image.

Targets

Known feature sizes

The bright discs within the phantom are 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 mm across. Watch the smallest ones disappear as voxel size grows.

About asymmetric sampling

This demo zero-fills one side of k-space to reveal the phase-sensitive artefact. Clinical partial-Fourier methods use conjugate symmetry plus phase estimation, so they reconstruct more accurately than this teaching approximation.
